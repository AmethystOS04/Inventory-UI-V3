combat guide

rift arena

the arena uses your selected weapon and artifact to survive waves of enemies

loadout

weapon and artifact

choose an unlocked weapon and an artifact before starting a run. weapon damage comes from its item catalog damage and rarity bonus. artifact damage uses artifact power when set, then falls back to item damage

controls

movement and attacks

move with wasd or the arrow keys. click or tap the arena to move toward a point. left click attacks with the weapon, and right click activates the equipped artifact

survival

health and difficulty

the player starts with a health bar. difficulty changes enemy health, speed, damage, and spawn rate. arena upgrades can improve damage, health, and rift resistance

progress

waves and rewards

defeated enemies count toward arena badge actions. event quests and weekly challenges use arena runs for their objectives and rewards

currencies

mission points

mp comes from missions and is used for selected upgrades and refreshes

stardust

stardust is a separate resource used for item imbues
